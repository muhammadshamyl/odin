Module 4.3 · 4.6 · 10.5

Quarantine

Structural failures only — a field that will not cast, a value out of range, a required field empty, a string over the 256-char cap. The whole row is diverted, never partially applied. Fixed rows re-enter as a new batch; there is no patch-in-place.

The 256-char cap is not cosmetic: one long, incompressible string in an indexed production column exceeds Postgres's btree entry limit and jams the bulk insert for the entire batch. Catching it per-row here defuses a one-row denial of service.
